Case study · Mood

Building the foundations for a scalable design system

Creating a shared design language across Figma, engineering, and AI-generated experiences.

Role
Design Systems Lead
Foundations, naming, and token rollout
Team
Design, Engineering
Platform
Figma, Shopify

Context

Mood did not have a shared design foundation, so features were being designed and built with different colors, typography, spacing, and naming conventions.

Before scaling the system, I focused on the foundations.

The goal was to create a shared language that design and engineering could agree on before introducing more components and patterns.

Before components, we needed a shared language.

The biggest problems were not missing components. They were inconsistencies underneath them.

01
Different values for the same visual purpose
02
Naming decisions made independently from engineering
03
Platform limitations discovered too late
04
Large changes difficult to validate across real screens

Three layers

01
Foundations
Color, type, spacing, opacity, and naming.
02
Token migration
Introduce the system gradually, starting with spacing and then color.
03
Platform constraints
Document where the product cannot reproduce the design exactly, and agree on the exception with engineering.

A design system still has to respect the platform.

Shopify Checkout

Shopify Checkout could not reproduce every design token exactly, including the original gradient and border radius.

Instead of treating this as a design failure, we documented the limitation and agreed on the closest supported implementation with engineering.

Outcome

The foundation and naming system were established, and spacing tokens were shipped into production.

Color tokens are being rolled out next.

What I learned

A design system is not only a collection of reusable values.

It is also an agreement between design, engineering, and the platform the product is built on.

The more clearly those constraints are documented, the easier it becomes for both people and AI systems to use the system consistently.
